Bicol Express

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Coconut Milk
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Alamang
  • Pork Belly
  • Chili's
  • Magic Sarap

Steps

  1. Sauté garlic, ginger, onion, pork and bagoong
  2. Pour coconut milk and simmer until pork is tender.
  3. Add chili and cook for another 10 minutes. Season with Magic Sarap. Transfer into a serving plate and serve. Taste.
