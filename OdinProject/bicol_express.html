Bicol Express

Ingredients

Ingredients Quantity Notes
Pork Belly 500 g Cut into small pieces
Gata 400 ml Full fat
Alamang 2 tbsp Adjust to Saltiness
Onion 1 Pc Chopped
Onion 1 Pc Quartered
Garlic 4 cloves Minced
Ginger 1 Pc Sliced
Chili 5-8 pcs Adjust to spice level
Vegetable Oil 2 tbsp For sauteing
Magic Sarap 1 tbsp Seasoning

Steps

  1. Saute garlic, ginger, onion, pork and bagoong
  2. Pour coconut milk and simmer until pork is tender.
  3. Add chili and cook for another 10 minutes. Season with Magic Sarap. Transfer into a serving plate and serve. Taste.
