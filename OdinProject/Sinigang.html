Sinigang na baboy

Ingredients

Ingredients Quantity Notes
Pork Belly 800 g Cut into small pieces
Water 8 cups For the broth
Sinigang Mix 1 pack Or fresh tamarind
Tomatoes 2 Pcs Quartered
Onion 1 Pc Quartered
Radish 1 Pc Sliced
Eggplant 2 Pcs Sliced
Sitaw 1 Bundle Cut into 2 inch pieces
Kangkong 1 Bunch Tender stems
Chili's 2 Pcs Optional
Fish sauce 2 tbsp Adjust to taste

Steps

  1. Simmer pork with water with onion and tomatoes for 45mins.
  2. Add siling panigang, radish, okra and sitaw with 2-min intervals.
  3. Pour sinigang mix and fish sauce.
  4. Stir in kangkong. Transfer into a serving bowl and serve immediately Taste.
