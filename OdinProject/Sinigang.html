Sinigang na baboy

Ingredients

  • water
  • Onion
  • radish
  • okra
  • siling panigang
  • kangkong
  • pork belly
  • sitaw
  • tomatoes
  • patis
  • sinigang mix

Steps

  1. Simmer pork with water with onion and tomatoes for 45mins.
  2. Add siling panigang, radish, okra and sitaw with 2-min intervals.
  3. Pour sinigang mix and fish sauce.
  4. Stir in kangkong. Transfer into a serving bowl and serve immediately Taste.
