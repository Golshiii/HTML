Pork Adobo

Ingredients

  • water
  • garlic
  • vinegar
  • oyster sauce
  • bay leaf
  • pork belly
  • vegetable oil
  • magic sarap
  • black peppercorn
  • brown sugar

Steps

  1. Combine pork, MAGGI Oyster Sauce, garlic, vinegar, water, peppercorn and bay leaf in a pot. Cover and simmer for 45mins. Strain pork and set aside
  2. Sauté pork and garlic in oil until light golden brown. Pour cooking liquid and season with MAGGI Magic Sarap. Simmer until sauce is thick and coating the pork.
  3. Transfer on a serving plate and serve. Taste.
