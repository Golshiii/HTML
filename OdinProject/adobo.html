Pork Adobo

Ingredients

Ingredients Quantity Notes
Pork Belly 800 g Cut into small pieces
Water 1 cup For simmering
Vinegar 1/3 cup Cane or white vinegar
Oyster sauce 3 tbsp Replaces soy sauce
Garlic 6 Cloves Crushed
Bay leaf 3 Pcs Dried
Black peppercorn 1 tsp Whole
Brown Sugar 1 tbsp Balance the vinegar
Vegetable Oil 2 tbsp for browning the pork
Magic Sarap 1 tbsp Seasoning

Steps

  1. Combine pork, MAGGI Oyster Sauce, garlic, vinegar, water, peppercorn and bay leaf in a pot. Cover and simmer for 45mins. Strain pork and set aside
  2. Sauté pork and garlic in oil until light golden brown. Pour cooking liquid and season with MAGGI Magic Sarap. Simmer until sauce is thick and coating the pork.
  3. Transfer on a serving plate and serve. Taste.
